# 🧸 Baby & Tummy Products Sales Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on baby and tummy product sales data to uncover meaningful business insights. The goal is to understand sales patterns, customer behavior, and product performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze sales trends over time
* Identify top-performing products
* Understand revenue distribution
* Detect patterns and anomalies in sales
* Provide data-driven business insights

---

## 📊 Dataset

* Dataset includes sales-related information such as:

  * Product details
  * Sales amount
  * Quantity sold
  * Categories
  * Dates
